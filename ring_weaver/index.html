Ring Weaver

The machine blows numbered bubbles that float up and stick to the ceiling. Two bubbles with the same number merge into one twice as big.

Three pipes in the ceiling each ask for one number. Get that exact bubble into the pipe to ship it: big points, and the space is free again. A wrong bubble sitting under a pipe blocks it.

Tap for a gentle puff. Hold to charge a hard shot that shoves the pile, to knock a bubble into a pipe or clear one that's in the way.

If the pile hangs below the red line for two seconds, the machine chokes. Orders grow with your biggest bubble, all the way to one million.
