Ring Weaver

The machine is puffing smoke rings and the pressure is climbing. Hold and drag to draw one unbroken strand through the rings. Lift to tie it off. Every finished pattern vents pressure.

Chain
Thread 2 or more rings in one strand. Points grow with the square of the count.
Harmony ×2
Every ring in the chain is the same colour (3+ rings).
Weave ×1.5
Turn left, right, left… at each ring (4+ rings).
Orbit ×1.5
Turn the same way at every ring (4+ rings).
Loop ×1.5
End the strand back where it started.
Double ×1.5
Pass through the same ring twice.
Knot
+25% for each time the strand crosses itself, up to 4.
Ember rings
Dark red rings snap the strand and spike the pressure.

Rings that drift off the top unthreaded add pressure. Reach 100 and the boiler blows.
