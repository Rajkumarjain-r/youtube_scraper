Slingshot your rocket from star to star and keep it fuelled. The aliens are right behind you.

    Finish all three to raise your score multiplier for good.

    How to play

    • TapLeave orbit along the dotted line. The label on the target star shows what that tap gets you.
    • FuelThe dots around a star are fuel cells. Land on it to refuel. Grey stars are empty.
    • ChainLand on the same colour again and again to multiply fuel and score.
    • CoinsCoins sit on part of each orbit. Circle longer to grab them, but every second burns fuel.
    • LassoCross your exhaust trail around stars to take them all at once. Crossing your trail is otherwise harmless.
    • HolesBlack holes (sector 2+) bend your path. The preview marks them PULL! Touch the core and you lose fuel.
    • BurnCaught by a black hole or drifting? Tap to fire your engine (−4 fuel) and blast to the nearest safe star.
    • AliensSlip up and their mothership rises from below and hunts you for 6 seconds. Another mistake while it hunts and the beam lunges for double. Play clean and it gives up. It also comes when your fuel runs low.
    • PickupsSome stars hold a pickup in their centre. Land on that star to grab it. JET autopilots you, MAG pulls in coins, 2× doubles score, SHLD blocks one mistake.

    The first 20 jumps are a warm-up. After that the pace rises every 20 jumps.
