Wake

Your comet is burning out, and the Devourer is climbing after it. Hop from star to star to keep your Light alive. The less Light you have, the closer it gets.

  • TapBreak orbit and fly along the dotted line. The preview shows what that tap will get you.
  • FuelLanding on a star absorbs its sparks (the dots) and refills your Light. Used stars go grey.
  • ChainLand on the same colour again and again to multiply everything you absorb.
  • LassoCross your own trail around stars to absorb them all at once. Crossing it around nothing snags you and costs Light.
  • DustFly through stardust streams for points and a little Light. The preview counts the dust (✦) on each shot.
  • PickupsJet autopilots you upward, Magnet pulls in dust, 2× doubles score, Shield blocks one mistake.
  • MistakesMisses, snags and hazards feed the Devourer. A second mistake within 4 seconds and it lunges for double.

The first 20 jumps are a warm-up. After that the pace climbs. Four boosters at the bottom help when you're stuck.
